A futures generator for workshops on AI, the future of work and customers' financial lives. Set five forces, tune a future and explore what it means for the people we serve.

How the desk works

Use it as provocation, not a forecast. Every figure links to a published report, and customer stories are illustrative composites with no real customer data.

  1. Step 01Set the forcesAnswer five questions about the world in 2050. Each has three choices, and there are no wrong answers.
  2. Step 02Tune a futureThe desk matches your answers to the nearest of eight futures, each built from several published reports.
  3. Step 03Explore itOpen three sub-futures to see how we get there, a customer living through it and a How Might We for financial services.
