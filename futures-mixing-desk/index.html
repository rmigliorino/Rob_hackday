    How the desk works

    The five questions are the big uncertainties.

    Generate matches your answers to the nearest of eight future archetypes, each drawn from several reports. Sub-futures are scored against your answers and three are drawn from the strongest. Timeline figures are sourced. Customer stories are composites written for the workshop, built on the evidence beside them, with no real customer data.

    Each future is tuned like a signal. Its object is a sound form (a scope trace, a spectrum, ripples, waveforms, frequency ridges or a speaker) generated from your answers, so every future gets its own specimen and frequency. Use the desk as provocation, not a forecast.
