How to read the desk

Observed evidencePublished projectionScenario assumptionStrategic hypothesis

Observed evidence is a sourced fact about a stated time and place; it is not proof of 2050. Published projections come from named reports with their own assumptions. Scenario assumptions are the conditions you chose. Strategic hypotheses are reasoned, contestable interpretations written for this desk.

All content is curated in advance; nothing is generated at run time and no data leaves your device. Customer moments are illustrative composites, not real people. This is a test slice: one financial need, three tensions and a provisional set of five conditions.
